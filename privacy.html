Zoop My Way privacy policy

Zoop My Way is a drawing puzzle game developed by Spooky Game Studios. Contact: spookygamestudios@gmail.com. This policy covers the release Android package com.zoopmyway.game. Development builds use a separate package and test advertising configurations.

Playing and information stored on your device

The campaign can be played offline without creating a player account. The game stores unlocked levels, preferences, a broad age-band choice and ad-pacing state on your device. Ad-pacing state includes successful clears, consecutive failed runs and active-play intervals. We do not ask for your date of birth, real name, location or phone number to play, and we do not send your date of birth to an advertising service. The app sends an age-restriction signal to Google to apply the appropriate ad treatment.

Younger players and unknown ages

The game is designed for a mixed audience, ages 6 and older. A neutral age question lets the game select privacy settings; it does not ask for a date of birth. There is no reward for selecting an adult age group. For all players under 18, players who prefer not to answer, and unanswered or invalid ages, the app requests child age treatment, a maximum general-audience ad content rating, and non-personalized ads. It signals under-age status to Google's consent platform so that children are not asked to consent as adults. Analytics and crash diagnostics remain disabled for these players.

Advertising

Advertising is enabled by default for all age groups. A local Ads-off preference saved in an earlier game version is respected; re-enabling it requires a deliberate choice in Privacy. The current game does not offer a new general Ads-off switch or a paid Remove Ads purchase.

For adults, Google's User Messaging Platform presents consent choices where required before advertising requests. The game checks Google's permission to request ads and does not bypass a failed privacy check. Google applies consent choices and applicable restrictions to determine which advertising may be requested, including personalized, non-personalized or limited ads where permitted. Rejecting personalized advertising does not necessarily turn all advertising off. Players can open Ad privacy choices from Settings → Privacy to review or withdraw choices where Google's privacy-options form is required. Children and unanswered ages receive the restricted treatment described above.

Google AdMob may process network addresses, app/device information, identifiers, consent choices and ad interactions to deliver ads, measure their performance and prevent fraud. The app declares Android's Advertising ID permission so Google AdMob can access the device's advertising identifier where permitted by the player's consent, Android advertising settings and the audience restrictions described above. AdMob applies child-directed treatment for players under 18 and unknown ages. Firebase Advertising ID collection remains disabled. Other identifiers or network information may also be processed; operating-system and SDK behavior can differ by Android version.

Full-screen ads appear only between campaign attempts, with opening protection and active-play spacing; never while drawing or driving. This release does not offer rewarded hint ads or banners. Development and Play-sandbox builds use Google's test ad units.

Optional analytics and crash diagnostics

Analytics and crash diagnostics are a separate adult choice and are off by default. When enabled, Firebase Analytics and Crashlytics may receive gameplay events, app and operating-system versions, device information, pseudonymous installation identifiers, event timing and crash stack traces. Gameplay events include starts, clears, failed runs, retries and ad rewards. We do not set a custom player name, email or advertising profile in analytics. Firebase advertising storage, ad user data, ad personalization and Advertising ID collection remain disabled.

Turning diagnostics off disables future application and SDK collection. An already-started request can finish, and turning a switch off does not delete information already received by Google. Crash reports can remain on the device and may be sent after a later opt-in. Provider retention and deletion also depend on project configuration and Google's policies; immediate deletion after opting out is not promised.

Purchases and payments

This release has no in-app purchases, subscriptions or paid Remove Ads option. The game does not collect payment details or send purchase receipts to a verification server.

Your choices, retention and contact

Adults can change the optional Diagnostics choice in Privacy. Ad privacy choices are available there for Google forms where required; a prior local Ads-off preference remains respected. The age choice stays on the installation; the app offers no incentive or immediate retry to change a child's answer. Device settings can clear local game storage. Provider-held data is separate from local storage and follows the providers' settings, legal requirements and policies. For questions or privacy requests, contact spookygamestudios@gmail.com. Do not send passwords, private keys or card details by email.

The developer website

The developer website is hosted on GitHub Pages without advertising, analytics scripts or cookies added by the developer. GitHub may log network addresses for security and hosting operations. See GitHub's privacy statement. The public app-ads.txt file identifies the authorized seller of the game's advertising inventory.
