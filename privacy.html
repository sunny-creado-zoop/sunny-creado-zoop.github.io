Zoop My Way privacy policy

Zoop My Way is a drawing puzzle game developed by Sunny Creado. Contact: sunnycreado100@gmail.com. This policy covers the release Android package com.zoopmyway.game. Development builds use a separate package and test advertising configurations.

Playing and information stored on your device

The campaign can be played offline without creating a player account. The game stores unlocked levels, preferences, a broad age-band choice and ad-pacing state on your device. Ad-pacing state includes successful clears, consecutive failed runs and active-play intervals. We do not ask for your date of birth, real name, location or phone number to play, and the game does not send your age-band choice to its optional services.

Younger players and unknown ages

The game can be played by a mixed audience including children. A neutral age question appears before optional services become available. The app does not enable ads, analytics or crash diagnostics for players under 18, players who prefer not to answer or players who have not answered. These players can still play the campaign. There is no reward for choosing an adult age band.

Advertising for adults

For an adult who has not previously saved a privacy choice, advertising is enabled by default. Where required, Google's User Messaging Platform presents consent choices before ad requests. Google applies those choices and the applicable rules to determine which ads may be requested, including personalized, non-personalized or limited ads where permitted. Rejecting personalization is not necessarily the same as turning all advertising off. Adults can turn the game's separate Ads choice off in Privacy; this stops new ad requests. Where required, Google's privacy-options form can also be reopened there.

Google AdMob may process network addresses, app/device information, identifiers, consent choices and ad interactions to deliver ads, measure their performance and prevent fraud. The app omits Android's Advertising ID permission and disables Firebase Advertising ID collection. This does not mean that no other identifiers or network information are processed; operating-system and SDK behavior can differ by Android version.

Automatic ads are considered only at ended campaign attempts, with opening protection and active-play spacing. A rewarded hint is an explicit choice and is granted only after the SDK confirms the reward. Development and Play-sandbox builds use Google's test ad units.

Optional analytics and crash diagnostics

Analytics and crash diagnostics are a separate adult choice and are off by default. When enabled, Firebase Analytics and Crashlytics may receive gameplay events, app and operating-system versions, device information, pseudonymous installation identifiers, event timing and crash stack traces. Gameplay events include starts, clears, failed runs, retries and ad rewards. We do not set a custom player name, email or advertising profile in analytics. Firebase advertising storage, ad user data, ad personalization and Advertising ID collection remain disabled.

Turning diagnostics off disables future application and SDK collection. An already-started request can finish, and turning a switch off does not delete information already received by Google. Crash reports can remain on the device and may be sent after a later opt-in. Provider retention and deletion also depend on project configuration and Google's policies; immediate deletion after opting out is not promised.

Purchases and payments

This release has no in-app purchases, subscriptions or paid Remove Ads option. The game does not collect payment details or send purchase receipts to a verification server.

Your choices, retention and contact

Adults can change the game's Ads and Diagnostics choices in Privacy. The age choice stays on the installation; the app offers no incentive or immediate retry to change a child's answer. Device settings can clear local game storage. Provider-held data is separate from local storage and follows the providers' settings, legal requirements and policies. For questions or privacy requests, contact sunnycreado100@gmail.com. Do not send passwords, private keys or card details by email.

The developer website

The developer website is hosted on GitHub Pages without advertising, analytics scripts or cookies added by the developer. GitHub may log network addresses for security and hosting operations. See GitHub's privacy statement. The public app-ads.txt file identifies the authorized seller of the game's advertising inventory.
